Common Questions

How do I get a quote?

Getting a quote is easy. Call us on 066 312 8522 or 062 002 7252, send us a WhatsApp message, or fill in our online contact form. We will reply promptly with a free, no-obligation quote.

What areas do you serve?

We cover Kraaifontein, Cape Town CBD, Northern Suburbs (Brackenfell, Bellville, Parow), Southern Suburbs, and the wider Western Cape. Contact us to confirm if we cover your specific area — we aim to accommodate all Cape Town area requests.

How much does furniture removal cost in Cape Town?

The cost depends on the volume of items, the distance of the move, and the specific services needed. We offer transparent, competitive pricing with no hidden costs. Contact us for a personalised free quote.

Do you offer rubble and garden refuse removal?

Yes. We provide efficient rubble removal and garden refuse clearing for residential properties, renovations and construction sites throughout Cape Town and Kraaifontein. All waste is disposed of responsibly.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking a few days in advance to secure your preferred date. For urgent moves, call us directly and we will do our best to accommodate you as soon as possible.

Do you do long-distance moves?

Yes. We handle long-distance moves within the Western Cape and to other South African provinces. Get in touch with your move details and we will provide a customised quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer). Payment terms will be confirmed when your booking is arranged.
