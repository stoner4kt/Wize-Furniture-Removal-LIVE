About Us

Your trusted removal partner in Cape Town and Kraaifontein

Your Trusted Transport Partner

Wize Furniture Removal is a Cape Town-based removal company proudly operating from Kraaifontein, serving homes, businesses and individuals across the Western Cape. We were built on a simple promise: treat every customer's belongings as our own, show up on time, and get the job done right at a fair price.

From our humble beginnings, we have grown into one of the area's most trusted removal services — known for our friendly team, careful handling and transparent quotes. Whether you're moving a one-bedroom flat or clearing a construction site, we bring the same level of dedication to every job.

We cover Kraaifontein, Brackenfell, Cape Town CBD, Northern Suburbs, Southern Suburbs and all surrounding Western Cape areas. No job is too small or too far.

Our Mission

To provide every client with a stress-free, professional and affordable removal experience — making every move simple from start to finish.

Our Values

Reliability, honesty and care guide everything we do. We are committed to transparent pricing, on-time service and the safe handling of your belongings.

Our Team

Our experienced, friendly team is trained in safe furniture handling, efficient loading and professional customer service — so you can relax on moving day.
